* 250g butter
* 1 cup caster sugar
* 1 cup brown sugar
* 1 teaspoon vanilla
* 2 eggs
* 3 cups SR Flour
* 1 cup rolled oats
* 100g choc chips

* * *

1. Preheat oven to a moderate 180°C.
2. Put the softened (at room temperature) butter, sugar, and vanilla into a bowl of a stand mixer and beat until well mixed.
3. Add flour one cup at a time.
4. Add oats, salt, and half of the chocolate chips, mix until incorporated.
5. Roll into small balls and flatten onto a baking tray.
6. Add 4-5 chocolate chips on top of each ball.
7. Bake for 12 mins or until golden brown.
8. Lightly sprinkle kosher/flake salt on top of each cookie whilst hot.
9. Cool on a wire rack until room temperature.
